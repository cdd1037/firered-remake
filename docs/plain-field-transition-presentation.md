# Ordinary field transition presentation

The accepted movement producer now supplies a transient typed cause and both endpoints. Ordinary connected edges bypass the old blanket map-change fade. This does not yet implement continuous cross-edge camera/player interpolation.

Immediate ordinary warps on NONE/SUNNY map headers in the same nonempty region section share departure/arrival controllers. The source `fldeff_flash.c` region-section condition proves that this bounded subset does not request a map preview. Plain and animated-door departure/arrival branches are independent. Animated branches additionally require stable walking-avatar origin and destination states and generated door art at every animated endpoint. Automatic non-animated door steps, stairs, forced movement, cave/preview and weather-specific branches remain outside this slice.

`FieldWarpPresentation` extracts the existing common-fall palette scheduler into one authority used by live warp, common-fall and recorded playback. The two palette banks update on separate callbacks. Departure waits for both palette completion and actual requested music-stop completion. An unchanged song is ready while still playing; absent/faulted audio cannot strand the cosmetic controller. Arrival follows the ordinary exit/weather/palette order. Both minimum departure and plain arrival consume 24 callbacks. The shared music adapter retains the existing source fade and admits its ordinary indoor/outdoor speeds 2/4.

The live view captures detached origin/destination field projections and uses the same map/actor/palette renderer as recap. Gameplay has already committed at the ordinary command boundary. Input and saves are locked during presentation; play-time observations continue and are committed after release. No movement, damage, RNG or map script is rerun by presentation.

## Bounded animated doors

`FieldDoorPresentation` follows the pinned `field_fadetransition.c`, `field_door.c`, normal held-walk, palette and barn-door-window callbacks. An ordinary door departure opens, walks north, hides the player, closes, requests the music/palette fade, then transfers after both readiness gates. Its minimum is 81 callbacks; the actual switch can be later when music is still stopping. Animated arrival consumes 81 callbacks: its delay-3 palette and outward window reveal begin before door opening at callback 26; the southward walk begins at callback 46, closing starts at callback 60 and overlaps the final two walking pixels, and release occurs at callback 81. Live and recorded playback use the same phase sampler, door frames, visibility, displacement and wipe values.

The art recipe selects the 23 real source small-door rows. This includes ordinary town houses, general normal/sliding doors and the dynamically unlocked Rocket Warehouse door. It excludes the source table's unused Fan Club art row: `field_door.c` explains that its metatile was reused for the non-door SS Anne harbor warp. The ordinary world importer derives bindings from each layout's actual tilesets and metatiles, and generates complete replacement BG layers for its three open frames plus the source-selected door sound. Runtime lookup uses `(layout, metatile)` before starting an animated transition; there is no map-name allowlist or invented fallback art. This covers the real 1×1 door art family, not the separate 1×2 elevator/cable-club/teleporter programs. The representative public-command route is Pallet Town's rival house: outdoor player `(15,8)` approaches door `(15,7)`, enters at room exit `(4,8)`, then returns through that south-arrow warp.

The normal held walk uses eight callbacks per foot/standing command. Arrival resets the source gait seed and is defined exactly. Departure inherits the general field renderer's missing alternating-foot command-index state; its cadence and displacement are defined, but the inherited choice of left versus right foot remains a fidelity limitation. This slice does not infer that state from a map or add it to historical recordings.

The accepted gameplay receipt remains authoritative. `From` is the tile south of a departure door. For arrival, `FieldMovementRules` has already committed `To` one tile south of the door; `ArrivalAnchor` is the authentic door cell. Detached arrival rendering starts at that anchor and walks to the existing `To`. Presentation never moves the saved player a second time. Origin-aware capability checks also prevent an indoor entry's eager bicycle/surf reset from disguising an unsupported origin mode.

## Recording and compatibility

Only the exact accepted receipt and its newly emitted provisional map marker can receive a completed observation. An exact same-command first-arrival caption may follow the marker; its producer payload and committed arrival flags are authenticated before admission. The observed switch/end callbacks are reconciled through the next existing accepted transaction, or projected into an immediate field save. Rejected commands do not publish it. No direct command-state setter or separate revision is introduced.

The optional timing retains its eight-byte logical base allocation. A non-plain arrival kind adds one logical byte; each optional typed departure/arrival door anchor adds four. These are bounded logical allocations, not JSON byte counts. Door anchors are populated only from the exact accepted receipt, with departure north-facing and arrival south-facing geometry validated against the current recorded segment player, including intervening movement. The arrival kind is independent of the departure cause. Plain timing keeps its existing serialized shape because default arrival and absent anchors are omitted.

Overflow closes recording with the whole unknown marker intact, rather than truncating an actual audio wait or retaining partial geometry. Older, unobserved and unsupported transitions remain unknown and switch immediately during replay; no cause or anchor is inferred from destination identity. Authentic same-map warps can produce a marker. Replay uses the same phase sampler and the recorded switch boundary, without consulting current audio. The authenticated first-arrival caption moves from the provisional one-frame marker end to the observed transition end, retaining its duration and payload. Input, movement, other caption groups and script tails remain outside this bounded admission.
