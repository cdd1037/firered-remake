# First-clear refactor and stop boundary

The 2026-10-12 instruction is to finish the first main story, then perform a bounded project-level refactor and simplification, synchronize source and the complete Drive package, clean reproducible temporary material, and pause for further instructions. It does not authorize beginning postgame content. This is a development stopping condition, not a new gameplay restriction on a valid completed save.

## Exit before refactoring

Require the actual eight-badge route through Victory Road and the first League, a Champion win, Hall of Fame record, durable completion save and a fresh Continue. Clearly distinguish source/logic checks, headless view checks and unavailable native visual/audio comparison. Remaining visual fidelity and regional service gaps stay explicit.

## Bounded changes

1. Consolidate repeated profile selection and save-upgrade routing in the tooling/launch paths. Keep exact profile boundaries, source content authentication and atomic new-destination publication. Existing valid save payloads and generations must remain unchanged except for already declared migrations.
2. Reuse region service adapters and test helpers where they perform the same work. Preserve distinct source scripts and meaningful business assertions. Do not replace trainer teams or encounter tables to simplify tests.
3. Remove completed one-off candidate/patch assembly scripts only after verifying that the permanent importers and their pinned inputs can reproduce the necessary resources. Keep source importers, rebuild contracts and the small evidence needed to understand accepted and unresolved behavior.
4. Review small measured costs, such as repeated expansion of immutable compact map cells. The Victory Road test planner repeatedly indexed `PretWorldMap.Cells`, whose getter expands compact cells on every access; its test-only fix caches the array once. Product callers must be checked before deciding whether a product cache is useful. Do not add a general caching framework or change map validation to claim a speedup.

Each accepted change needs a normal build and a few affected checks. Do not rerun historical routes, every battle matrix, or backup restore environments. No arbitrary file/line-count target and no wholesale battle or catalog rewrite.

## Final delivery

Record the resulting source commit, required author inputs, reconstruction contracts, first-clear checkpoint and unresolved feature list. GitHub receives the normal private source increment. The complete Drive package is checked for structural and byte integrity under the current backup policy; it does not trigger another reconstructed runtime/replay exercise. Delete only identified rebuildable caches or confirmed duplicate copies, preserving unique sources, checkpoints and evidence. Then stop development pending the user's next instruction.
